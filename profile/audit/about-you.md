## How the strategist uses this file

- **Pillars:** derived from work × story × audit material × continuous learning from transcripts. NOT from a hypothesis guess — synthesis proposes from data.
- **Audience-of-one:** Q3 produces the audience.md test
- **Cadence + format:** constrained by Q1 personal context (time, energy)
- **Voice:** Q3 (dinner-party) is one of the highest-fidelity voice samples — mined during synthesis
- **No-list:** Q5 seeds it directly; transcripts and grill encounters grow it over time
- **Money path:** Q6 multi-select tags each pillar at synthesis
- **90-day picture:** derived from Q2 12-month answer (90-day is the ~25% mark toward that)
